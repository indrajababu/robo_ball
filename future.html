Future Improvements

What we'd build next given more time.

Hardware

  • Faster actuators / lower-latency drivers
  • Improved end-effector geometry for catching reliability
  • Stiffer frame to reduce vibration

Perception

  • Stereo or depth camera for direct 3D ball position
  • Learned ball detector robust to lighting/background
  • Multi-ball tracking

Planning & control

  • Model-predictive control with online dynamics learning
  • Reinforcement learning for tossing policy
  • Recover gracefully from missed catches

Stretch goals

Two-ball juggling, juggling against a human partner, or juggling balls with different masses.
